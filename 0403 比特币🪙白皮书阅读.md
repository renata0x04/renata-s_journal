In 2008, Satoshi Nakamoto published the bitcoin white book on the p2p foundation website. It has been over 17 yrs, and time has proven bitcoin's value as *digital gold*.

So, as long as I have read it before, this is also a good time to review the main idea and the principles of bitcoin.

Let's go back to 2008, the global financial crisis occurred,  the vulnerabilities of traditional banking system has exposed. Two weeks after Lheman Brothers' bankruptcy,  Bitcoin white-paper announced that a new peer-to-peer electronic cash system, which without any trusted third party. And this allows any two willing parties to transact directly with each other, without the need for a trusted third party. Just like in China, Alipay, WechatPay and other e-market platforms serve for security.

The system of bitcoin based on cryptographic proof. It define an electronic coin as a chain of digital signatures. When a transaction happens, a hash of previous transaction and the public key of the next owner will be added to the end of coin.  While the problem is the payee don't know if this coin has been double-spend. So, it imported a mint based model knows all transactions, and all transactions would be publicly announced. Then he introduced a timestamp server, each timestamp includes the former timestamp, and one step by one, they form a chain.

But in a decentralised environment,  who publish and broadcast timestamp? (we can't rely on a trusted third party) How to ensure chronological reliability? (in case someone create parallel chains) How to reach a consensus? (some node may produce different block versions, and which one is true? )

So we need PoW, proof-of-work, increasing real electricity and computing costs, to reach a decentralised consensus. Consequently, once a block is added into the chain by PoW, and the latter would add behind it. The cost of tampering with a blockchain is extremely high. 

And then all nodes run in a network(contains six steps, omitted here), and transactions is not reversible. 

These above is the main idea of the bitcoin system, in the original text, the second half further provided some technique solutions for its implementation.
(omitted here)

- Section 9, Combining and Splitting Value, to improve transaction efficiency.
- Section 10, bitcoin is not fully anonymous, public keys as addresses.
- Section 11, Computing, the cost of attack is pretty high, demonstrated its safety again.
